# Couples

The couple gets a private login from their studio: one login per event, shared by both of them.

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Where do we log in? | Use the link your studio sent (it looks like live.mcreatik.com/client/login?event=your-event) with the email and temporary password. If you have more than one gallery, you choose which one after you log in. You choose your own password the first time. |
| Can we add it to our phone like an app? | Yes. In Chrome choose "Add to Home screen", or in Safari use Share → Add to Home Screen. It opens as "My Photos". |
| We forgot our password. | Tap "Forgot password?" and enter your email. This works for all your galleries. You'll get a temporary password from connect@mcreatik.com that works for 30 minutes; check spam too. Your old password keeps working until you use the new one. |
| Can both of us use it? | Yes, with the same login on as many phones as you like. Changing the password logs out every other device. |
| How do we choose photos for our album? | Tap the + on any photo. The counter shows how many you've picked out of your album limit. |
| Is there a limit on album photos? | Only if your studio set one (for example 60). You can't pick more than the limit, but you can untick and swap at any time. |
| Does the studio see our picks? | Yes, straight away, and they can download your picks as a ZIP to design the album. |
| Can we download the full-quality photos? | Yes. Your studio uploads your finished photos here in full quality, and you download them as they are. |
| Can we download everything at once? | Yes. "Download all" or a category, or tap "Select photos to download" to pick some. A ZIP holds up to 1,000 photos, so very large events download in parts (by category). A percentage shows while it downloads; keep the page open. |
| How are photos organised? | By the studio's categories (Couple, Family, Rituals, Candid and so on). Use the tabs at the top. |
| Some photos we saw at the event are missing. | Guests see the live photos from the day. Your gallery holds the finished photos your studio delivers, so it can look different. Ask your studio if you're looking for a specific one. |
| Our gallery is empty. | Your studio adds your finished photos after the event. Ask them when they will be ready. |
| Who else can see our photos? | Guests with the QR code see their own gallery of smaller copies. Only you and your studio can open your login and your full-quality photos. |
| How long can we access them? | The gallery stays open for the months your studio chose (3 months by default). The photos are deleted 30 days after it closes. Download everything you want to keep before then. **(Ask your studio for the exact date.)** |
| It says the gallery is unavailable. | Access has been switched off for now. Contact your studio. |
| Can we remove a photo guests can see? | Ask your studio: they can hide it from guests instantly. |
