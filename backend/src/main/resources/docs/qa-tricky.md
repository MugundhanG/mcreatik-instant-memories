# Tricky questions

These are the objections that decide a sale. Answer calmly, and don't promise more than the app does.

| They say | Suggested reply |
| --- | --- |
| "Our family won't like their photos online." | The gallery isn't public: only people with your QR code can open it, it's hidden from Google, and location is removed from every photo. Anyone can ask for a photo to be taken down, and the studio can hide any photo instantly. |
| "Guests will share bad photos of us." | The studio controls what appears and can hide or delete any photo during the event; it disappears from every phone at once. |
| "What if the internet fails at the venue?" | Photos queue safely on the laptop and upload when the connection returns, and guests' phones catch up by themselves. A mobile hotspot works as a backup. |
| "What if your server goes down on the wedding day?" | Photos are stored and delivered by Cloudflare, and the camera uploads queue on the laptop, so nothing is lost. If the gallery is briefly unreachable, it carries on where it stopped. **(Don't promise an uptime figure; we don't measure one yet.)** |
| "Why not just use Google Drive?" | Drive shows photos after the event, needs sorting, and exposes everyone's Google accounts. This is live during the event, branded with your studio, needs no login for guests, and lets the couple pick album photos in the same place. |
| "Guests will stop hiring their own photographers / take our photos for free." | Guests get phone-quality copies with your name and Instagram on the page: it's free advertising to every guest. Full-quality originals stay with you and the couple. |
| "It's too expensive." | Ask: how many weddings a month, and what do you charge? One booking won from a guest who saw your branded gallery covers months of use. Offer the per-event option for low volume. |
| "My couple doesn't want it." | Turn it off for that event: McreatiK can switch the guest gallery off while you keep using the album selection and downloads. |
| "Is this legal under privacy law?" | We collect nothing that identifies guests, honour removal requests, and keep data only until the delete date. Our privacy notice is at live.mcreatik.com/privacy. **(For a legal opinion, refer them to their own advisor; don't give legal advice.)** |
| "Can you guarantee photos appear in seconds?" | Usually within seconds of the shot, depending on the camera's Wi-Fi and the venue internet. Do a test at the venue before guests arrive. |
| "What happens to our photos if McreatiK shuts down?" | You always keep your originals, and the couple can download everything as ZIPs at any time before the delete date. **(Agree a notice period before promising one.)** |
