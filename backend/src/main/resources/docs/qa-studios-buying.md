# Studios: buying and trust

These come up once a studio is interested. Lead every pricing conversation to a demo call.

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| How much does it cost? | A Main event (weddings, receptions, big functions) is ₹2,499; a Quick event (up to about 200 guests) is ₹1,499. Monthly packs bring it down to ₹1,250 per Main event or ₹1,000 per Quick event. Let's do a 10-minute demo and I'll suggest the cheapest option for your volume. |
| What's the difference between Main and Quick? | The features are the same. Quick is for smaller events (about 200 guests). If a Quick event turns out bigger, the gallery never stops; you can upgrade it to Main for ₹1,000. |
| What if I don't use all my pack's events? | They carry over to the next month (one month only). |
| What if I need more events than my pack has? | Extra events cost less while your pack is paid up: ₹999 on Main 4 and ₹1,499 on Main 2 (for Main or Quick events), ₹799 on Quick 4 and ₹999 on Quick 2 (Quick events). |
| Do I need two packs for weddings and engagements? | No. A Main pack covers Quick events too. |
| I only want a private gallery for the couple, no live event. | Use a **client gallery**: ₹250 per gallery plus storage at ₹15 per GB a month. A full wedding (about 2,000 photos with originals, ~25 GB) kept for 3 months costs ₹1,375 (₹250 + 25 GB × ₹15 × 3). You set your own price to the couple. |
| How much storage do I need? | About 80 full-quality photos per GB. Live events include 5 GB; most full weddings need 20 to 30 GB if you also upload originals. The dashboard shows the photos and GB as you go. |
| What happens if a live event goes over 5 GB? | Nothing stops on the day. You have 14 days after the event to add the extra GB (₹15 per GB a month, for the months the gallery has left), or the couple's gallery is paused until you do. |
| How do I pay? | In the dashboard: Events → Buy events (or Renew pack). Pay by UPI QR and enter the transaction ID (UTR). It's added straight away. |
| Can I try it first? | Yes. We'll run a live demo with you, and you can open the sample gallery on your phone. **(Decide whether to offer a free first event.)** |
| How long does setup take? | Creating an event takes a minute. Connecting a camera takes about 10 minutes the first time; we help with the first event. |
| Do I need technical skills? | No. Create the event, print the QR poster, paste the connection code into the uploader. We set it up with you the first time. |
| Who owns the photos? | You and your couple. We only store and show them for your event. We don't use them for anything else, show ads or sell data. |
| Can McreatiK see my photos? | Only to provide support when you ask, and to act on removal requests. We never use or share them. |
| Where are photos stored? Is it secure? | With Cloudflare (photos) and Supabase (data), both encrypted, over HTTPS. Originals are private: only you and the couple can download them, through links that expire within 30 minutes. Share the security page at live.mcreatik.com/privacy. |
| How long are photos kept? | Every gallery is open 3 months, about 90 days (from the event date, or from creation for a client gallery). Keep it open for as many more months as you choose, at ₹15 per GB of its storage per month. A closed gallery's photos are deleted for good 30 days later (the event record is kept); we email you 14 days before it closes. |
| Can I use my own website address? | Not today. Galleries live at live.mcreatik.com with your studio's name, logo and Instagram on them. |
| Can several staff use one studio account? | Today each studio has one login. It works on several devices, but a password change logs out the others. |
| How many guests can view at once? | It's built for big weddings: photos load from Cloudflare's network, not our server. **(Confirm a tested number before quoting one.)** |
| What if something goes wrong at the event? | Message us on WhatsApp at +91 99527 58545 or email connect@mcreatik.com. Uploads queue safely on the laptop meanwhile. |
| Can I cancel? | Yes. Galleries stay online until their delete date. **(Confirm refund terms before promising.)** |
