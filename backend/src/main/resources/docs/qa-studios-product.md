# Studios: product

These are the questions photographers ask when they want to know how it works on the day.

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| How do photos get from my camera to guests? | The camera sends each photo over Wi-Fi (FTP) to a laptop running the Instant Memories uploader. The laptop makes a light, sharp copy and uploads it, and it appears in the guest gallery within seconds. |
| Which cameras work? | Any camera with FTP transfer (Wi-Fi/FTP upload), for example Canon or Sony. Without FTP, the uploader can also watch a folder, such as a card-reader or tethering folder. |
| Can several photographers upload to one event? | Yes. Add one uploader per camera or laptop. Each gets its own connection code, and the dashboard shows which cameras are online and how many photos each sent. For a freelancer, press Add camera & get code and WhatsApp them the code and the uploader download link. Press Get new code to end their access. |
| What if the venue internet drops? | Nothing is lost. The laptop saves every photo to its queue first and uploads when the connection returns. Guests' phones catch up automatically. |
| Does it upload my full RAW or JPEG files? | By default only a light gallery copy (about 1 MB) is sent during the event, so it stays fast on venue internet. Originals stay on your card and laptop; you can upload full-quality finals later to the Client gallery from the dashboard. RAW files aren't supported (JPEG and PNG only). |
| Can I upload edited photos after the event? | Yes. In the dashboard open Photos → Client gallery and upload your edited, full-quality photos. Only the couple sees them. The Guest gallery tab is for the guests' compressed photos. Each tab has its own categories. On events made before 11 Nov 2026 the couple still sees every photo. |
| Do guests and the couple see the same photos? | No, on new events. Guests see the Guest gallery (camera photos, compressed). The couple sees the Client gallery (your full-quality finals). |
| I turned a gallery off. Did I lose uploads? | No. Uploads are still saved. Nobody sees them until you turn the gallery on. |
| Does the same photo upload twice? | No. Duplicates are recognized and skipped. |
| A guest asked to remove a photo. How do I find it? | Their email names the camera file, for example IMG_1234.JPG. Type it in the Search file name box on the Photos tab, then hide or delete it. |
| Can I remove a bad photo from guests' phones? | Yes. Hide or delete it and it disappears from open galleries immediately. In Select mode you can hide, like or delete many at once. |
| How do I give guests the link? | Every event has a unique QR code. Print your studio board once, then for each event a small event card for the board and table cards, all with your logo and design. You can also download the QR code alone. You can also copy the link. |
| Does the gallery show my brand? | Yes: your studio name, logo and Instagram on the gallery, the studio board and the cards. Set them in Account. |
| How does the couple get their photos? | Create their login under the Couple tab and send it on WhatsApp in one tap. They pick album photos and download your full-quality finals; you see their picks live. |
| Can I see how many guests used it? | Yes. The Guests tab shows people, QR scans, photo views, downloads, likes, peak hour, and the Most loved, Most downloaded and Most viewed photos. Guests stay anonymous. Guests can also tap Get HD photo and leave an email or WhatsApp number. Those requests show on the Guests tab with a WhatsApp or email link and a Done tick, so they work as booking leads. |
| Can I sort photos into albums? | Yes. Each event starts with categories (Couple, Family & Groups, Rituals, Cake cutting, Main event, Candid), and you can add your own. Guests and couples browse by them. Guest gallery and Client gallery each have their own list. |
| What does each event status do? | Draft: hidden. Upcoming: guests can open it, and it turns Live with the first camera photo. Live: photos stream in. Completed: the event is over but the gallery stays. Archived: hidden from guests and uploads stop. |
| What does the laptop need? | A Windows laptop or a Mac with the McreatiK Instant Memories Uploader installed (one download from the dashboard; Java is included), on the same Wi-Fi or hotspot as the camera, with internet for uploading. Do a short rehearsal with the laptop and camera before the first event. |
