# Studios: product

These are the questions photographers ask when they want to know how it works on the day.

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| How do photos get from my camera to guests? | The camera sends each photo over Wi-Fi (FTP) to a laptop running the Instant Memories uploader. The laptop makes a light, sharp copy and uploads it, and it appears in the guest gallery within seconds. |
| Which cameras work? | Any camera with FTP transfer over Wi-Fi (for example the Canon EOS R6 Mark II). Without FTP, the uploader can also watch a folder, such as a card-reader or tethering folder. |
| Can several photographers upload to one event? | Yes. Add one uploader per camera or laptop. Each gets its own connection code, and the dashboard shows which cameras are online and how many photos each sent. |
| What if the venue internet drops? | Nothing is lost. The laptop saves every photo to its queue first and uploads when the connection returns. Guests' phones catch up automatically. |
| Does it upload my full RAW or JPEG files? | By default only a light gallery copy (about 1 MB) is sent during the event, so it stays fast on venue internet. Originals stay on your card and laptop; you can add full-quality originals later from the dashboard. RAW files aren't supported (JPEG and PNG only). |
| Can I upload edited photos after the event? | Yes. In the dashboard: Photos → Upload, either compressed (fast, for the gallery) or originals (full quality, for the couple). You can pick a category before uploading. |
| Does the same photo upload twice? | No. Duplicates are recognized and skipped. |
| Can I remove a bad photo from guests' phones? | Yes. Hide or delete it and it disappears from open galleries immediately. In Select mode you can hide, like or delete many at once. |
| How do I give guests the link? | Every event has a unique QR code. Download it, or print the ready-made poster with your logo, and place it at the entrance and on tables. You can also copy the link. |
| Does the gallery show my brand? | Yes: your studio name, logo and Instagram on the gallery and the poster. Set them in Account. |
| How does the couple get their photos? | Create their login under the Couple tab and send it on WhatsApp in one tap. They pick album photos and download originals; you see their picks live. |
| Can I see how many guests used it? | Yes. The Guests tab shows people, QR scans, photo views, downloads, peak hour and the most-loved photos. Guests stay anonymous. |
| Can I sort photos into albums? | Yes. Each event starts with categories (Couple, Family & Groups, Rituals, Cake cutting, Main event, Candid), and you can add your own. Couples browse by them. |
| What does each event status do? | Draft: hidden. Upcoming: guests can open it, and it turns Live with the first camera photo. Live: photos stream in. Completed: the event is over but the gallery stays. Archived: hidden from guests and uploads stop. |
| What does the laptop need? | A Windows laptop or a Mac with the McreatiK Uploader installed (one download from the dashboard; Java is included), on the same Wi-Fi or hotspot as the camera, with internet for uploading. Windows is field-tested; **Mac: confirm with a rehearsal before promising.** |
