## Scene 1: Hook (0:00 to 0:17)
- **Goal:** name the pain (guests wait days for photos) and land the promise: "Every moment is live."
- **Visuals:** 0:00 to 0:03 logo animation on brand paper (#faf9f7). Then soft text cards over slow-moving demo photos (blurred, dark overlay). Last 4 s: the line "Every moment is live." with a red LIVE dot pulse (the app's live badge style), then a hard cut-in to the product.
- **On-screen text:** "Guests ask: when do we get the photos?" then "Usually, days." then "Every moment is live."
- **Footage:** motion graphics only, plus demo photos. No app UI yet.

## Scene 2: Studio dashboard (0:17 to 0:34)
- **Goal:** show that everything lives in one simple dashboard under the studio's own brand.
- **Visuals:** `/admin` events list of "Lens & Light Studio" with the plan summary at the top. Gentle zoom on the studio logo and name top right. Cut to the Account page: logo, studio name, Instagram, WhatsApp number fields.
- **On-screen text:** "One dashboard for every event". Callout on Account: "Your logo, name and WhatsApp, set once".
- **Note:** the sign-in itself is brief; the password is typed off camera.

## Scene 3: Create an event, board and QR (0:34 to 0:55)
- **Goal:** show it takes about a minute and produces print-ready, branded material.
- **Visuals:** New event form: Main selected, name, date. Event page Overview with the Guest QR code card. Open the Event & table cards page: event card, table card with studio logo and QR. Quick glimpse of the studio board (Account, Studio board).
- **On-screen text:** "Main for weddings, Quick for smaller functions". "Print-ready cards with your logo".

## Scene 4: Uploader (0:55 to 1:14)
- **Goal:** prove the camera-to-gallery path is simple and safe.
- **Visuals:** Dashboard Overview, Cameras & uploaders: camera shows Online (green dot) and photo count. Cut to the desktop Uploader, Live Upload page: status Online, camera strip with several cameras, a photo arriving (Recent photos list, pipeline turns green Camera, Computer, Upload server, Live gallery). Split screen: uploader on the left, guest phone on the right with the photo appearing.
- **On-screen text:** "Up to 4 cameras, one laptop". "Offline? Photos wait and upload later".

## Scene 5: Guest experience (1:14 to 1:39)
- **Goal:** the emotional core: guests scan, see photos live, interact, and can reach the studio.
- **Visuals:** phone frame (390x844) centred in the 16:9 canvas, soft blurred gallery background. A phone camera scans the QR from the table card (use the event card image on screen, then the gallery opens). LIVE badge, studio name and logo, photo grid, a new photo drops in at the top. Open a photo full screen, tap the heart (Like), tap Download. Show "Get HD photo". Scroll to the "Book Lens & Light Studio for your event" bar with WhatsApp and Call.
- **On-screen text:** "No app. No sign-up." "New photos appear within seconds". "Every guest is a possible client".

## Scene 6: Client gallery (1:39 to 2:00)
- **Goal:** show the professional handover: full-quality finals, album picks, ZIP.
- **Visuals:** Dashboard Photos tab with "Client gallery" tab, finals uploaded. Switch to phone frame: client login, My album, tap "+" on photos, counter "n / limit selected", Download original. Back to dashboard Couple tab: Album selection list with Download all (.zip).
- **On-screen text:** "Full-quality finals, only for the couple". "You see their picks at once".

## Scene 7: Results (2:00 to 2:18)
- **Goal:** show measurable value: reach, loved photos, leads.
- **Visuals:** Guests tab: people, scans, views, downloads, Likes, Contacted you; hourly chart; Most loved row; HD photo requests with WhatsApp link and Done tick (contact details masked).
- **On-screen text:** "See what guests loved". "HD requests become enquiries".

## Scene 8: Pricing and call to action (2:18 to 2:38)
- **Goal:** price clarity and a single next step.
- **Visuals:** `/pricing` page, scrolled to Main and Quick prices (zoom on ₹2,499 and ₹1,499). Then the end card on brand paper: logo, "Every moment is live.", "Book your free live demo", mcreatik.com, WhatsApp +91 99527 58545.
- **On-screen text:** "Main ₹2,499, Quick ₹1,499, monthly packs lower the price". End card text as above.
