# Positioning

## Problem
At Indian weddings and events, guests ask for photos on the day, but studios deliver days or weeks later. Guests share blurry phone pictures instead. Studios lose a free chance to show their work to a room full of future clients.

## Target customer
Photography studios in India that shoot weddings, receptions, engagements and other functions. Owner-photographers and small teams (one to several cameras, a laptop, an editor), who care about their brand, referrals and the next booking. Buyer: the studio owner. Users: the owner and the camera operator.

## Positioning statement
For photography studios in India who want their work seen and booked at the event itself, McreatiK Instant Memories is a live QR photo gallery that puts every shot on guests' phones within seconds, under the studio's own name, logo and WhatsApp number. Unlike sharing albums days later, it turns every guest into a possible next client.

## Five key selling points
1. **Live in seconds.** Photos shot at the venue appear in the guests' gallery within seconds; guests need no app or sign-up, just a QR scan.
2. **Your brand everywhere.** Your logo, name, Instagram and WhatsApp on the gallery, the studio board and the printed cards, plus a "Book ... for your event" bar.
3. **Easy for the camera team.** Any camera with Wi-Fi/FTP, one laptop for up to four cameras, and photos queue safely if the venue internet drops.
4. **A better handover to the couple.** A private gallery with full-quality finals, album picks with an optional limit that the studio sees at once, and ZIP downloads.
5. **Measurable enquiries.** Guest views, downloads, Likes, "Contacted you" taps, most-loved photos and HD photo requests that arrive as leads with a WhatsApp or email link.
